About the Game

Hello 👋 Himanshu this side please check and play every mode and enjoy gaming thank you Brain Trainer is a high-octane reflex and cognitive training arcade game. Designed to test your reaction speed, spatial memory, and mental processing, the game features dynamic animations, haptic feedback, and a highly competitive progression system.

Game Modes & Rules

  • Endless (Speed+): Survive as long as you can. The speed increases with your streak.
  • Time Attack (30s): Race against the clock to score as many points as possible in 30 seconds.
  • Perfect Streak (1 Life): A single mistake ends the run. How far can you go?
  • Math Master: Solve the arithmetic problem at the top. Tap the pillar with the correct answer!
  • Vowel Hunt: Look for the VOWEL (A, E, I, O, U) among the consonants. Tap it quickly!
  • Color Clash (Stroop Effect): Ignore the written word — tap the pillar matching the INK COLOR. Beware of the deceptive word trap!
  • Night Vision: Pillars will flash briefly then go dark. Memorize the target and tap it in the dark!
  • Sequence Memory (Simon Pro): Listen and watch the melodic pattern. Repeat the exact sequence! Each round adds +1 step to test your limits.

Features & Controls

Controls

Simply tap or click on the three interactive pillars on the screen based on the current mode's prompt. The game is optimized for both desktop mice and mobile touch screens.

Shop & Upgrades

Earn coins by maintaining high combo streaks. Spend your coins in the shop on consumable power-ups like Extra Lives, Shields, Coin Multipliers, Hyper Speed, or Chaos Mode. Permanent upgrades are also available.

Customization & Accessibility

Unlock up to 30 custom visual themes (including Mint, Cyberpunk, Void, and Neon Red). The game fully supports Colorblind mode, togglable sound effects, and device vibration haptics.
